Aim:
To find the largest rectangular area that can be formed in a histogram using a Monotonic Stack approach in O(n) time.

Objectives:
To understand the histogram largest rectangle problem.
To learn the concept of the Monotonic Stack data structure.
To implement an efficient O(n) algorithm for finding the maximum rectangular area.
To compare the brute-force approach with the optimized stack-based approach.
To develop problem-solving skills using stack-based algorithms.

Learning Outcomes:
Explain the Largest Rectangle in Histogram problem and its constraints.
Apply the Monotonic Stack technique to solve array-based problems efficiently.
Implement the largest rectangle algorithm in C++.
Analyze and compare the time and space complexities of brute-force and optimized approaches.
Recognize and apply monotonic stack concepts to related problems such as Maximal Rectangle, Trapping Rain Water, and Daily Temperatures.
